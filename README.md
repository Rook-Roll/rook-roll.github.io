# Rook & Roll — chess that rocks 🎸

A free chess training site that runs entirely in the browser — no sign-up. Coached by **Rocco**, a rock-star rook.

- **The Setlist** — 13 interactive lessons, from how the pieces move to forks, pins and mating patterns
- **Jam Session** — 25 tactics puzzles with hints and an adaptive puzzle rating
- **Live Show** — pass & play with a friend on one device (with clocks), or play Rocco — including an **Adaptive** level that learns your strength
- **Arcade** — Secret Queen, Pawn Wars, Coordinate Rush and Knight Sprint
- **Rocco** — chats with you, reviews your moves, gives hints and tips, and can speak out loud
- **Customize** — board colours, piece styles and colours, piece size and display options

Progress is saved in your browser. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step: open `index.html` to run it locally.
