Rook & Roll — free chess lessons, puzzles and games

Rook & Roll is a free chess training site for every level. Learn the rules step by step, solve tactics puzzles, play a friend on one device, and get coached by Rocco, a rock-star rook who reviews your moves and adapts to your skill.

Chess lessons for beginners to advanced players

26 interactive lessons: how the pieces move, check and checkmate, castling and en passant, piece values, not hanging pieces, opening principles, forks, pins and skewers, discovered attacks, double check, removing the defender, mating patterns, king and pawn endgames, forcing lines, quiet moves, sacrifices and mate in three.

235 chess puzzles

Tactics puzzles rated from 430 to 1850, filterable by level and theme: mate in 1, mate in 2, mate in 3, forks, back-rank mates, smothered mates, sacrifices and endgames. Your puzzle rating goes up as you solve.

Play chess with a friend on one device

Pass and play on the same phone, tablet or computer, with chess clocks and an auto-flipping board — or play the computer coach on Beginner, Casual, Club, Strong or Adaptive level.

Chess mini-games

Secret Queen, Pawn Wars, Coordinate Rush and Knight Sprint — fun ways to sharpen board vision and tactics.
